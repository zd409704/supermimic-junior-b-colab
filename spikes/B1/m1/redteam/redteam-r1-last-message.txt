1. **BLOCKER — M1.0, M1.5: δ = 0.03 lacks the claimed independent justification.** Its calibration uses observed rewards from the outcome-selected `failed=1` subset. Pooling arms does not make this independent of results. Moreover, the supposed constant offset becomes completion-dependent under R_full: multiplying rewards by survival fraction makes those terms contribute differently when survival differs. Subtracting their v1.0 offset therefore does not calibrate a margin on R_full. A percentage AULC gain also does not establish an equivalent percentage increase in learning speed. **Fix:** supply an external practical rationale for δ, or explicitly designate it an exploratory, data-informed threshold with no “20% faster-learning” interpretation.

2. **MAJOR — M1.5: The amendment does not establish whether its criterion raises or lowers the bar.** For positive scratch mean, the PLAN’s required difference is `0.2·mean(s)`; M1’s is `max(0.03, 2·SE(x,s))`. Either can exceed the other. Adding an SE floor does not necessarily make the replacement stricter. Likewise, δ = 0.10 is only a calibration at the stated old-scale reference value, not an equivalent PLAN threshold on R_full. **Fix:** state this algebraic comparison explicitly, describe S-δ10 as a fixed-margin sensitivity, and reserve claims about criterion equivalence for S-ratio.

3. **MAJOR — M1.2–M1.3: The metric identity and window language exceed what the benchmark establishes.** The identity with `Σr/(max_r·F)` holds when `steps ≤ F`; clipping the multiplier with `min` cannot remove rewards beyond F. M1.6 protects the primary calculation, but M1.3 simultaneously says `steps ≥ 5336` counts as complete and that `steps > 5336` is invalid. Also, `benchmark.py` accumulates the reward and increments `steps` before checking termination: the stored 5336-step return includes the terminating transition. It does not establish a clean reference-frame window excluding the defect. **Fix:** define the estimand as the first W benchmark transitions, explicitly including the terminal transition; make `steps = W` complete and `steps > W` non-computable; state the identity only on its valid domain. Qualify numerical reconstruction as subject to the benchmark’s stored floating-point precision.

4. **MAJOR — M1.0, M1.3: An observed common failure location is being promoted to a reference defect.** The pooled cutoff applies uniformly across arms, but its selection remains performance-dependent. Common failure across policies does not establish unattainability, which the amendment itself acknowledges is unverified. “Earliest window that is exact” is a computational justification, not a scientific justification for excusing failure. **Fix:** describe W as an exploratory, observed common-failure cutoff conditional on H2; remove categorical “reference-defect frame” language. Keep any interpretation that excuses policy failure explicitly conditional on H2 and the S-full result.

5. **BLOCKER — M1.3, M1.5–M1.6: Sensitivity validity and blocked-case execution are underspecified.** “Under S-excl dance1 is ignored” could waive only the horizon check or also the four-entry, numeric, frame and summary checks. It is also unclear whether S-full/S-excl are evaluated on their independently valid runs or only primary-valid runs, and whether a primary BLOCKED result stops sensitivities that remain computable. These choices can change reported outcomes. **Fix:** specify a validity matrix for each sensitivity, separating source-integrity checks from metric computability. Freeze scored attempts/executions first; compute each sensitivity’s validity, eligible seeds, verdict and partial outcome independently. Explicitly prohibit fallback execution selection after an M1 validation failure.

6. **MAJOR — M1.5: “Robust” excludes a directly relevant registered sensitivity.** S-ratio may disagree while the primary is still labelled robust. The one-factor sensitivities also leave interactions between clip handling and criterion untested, so the unqualified label overstates coverage. Undefined or BLOCKED sensitivity outcomes have no explicit interpretation. **Fix:** require all four listed sensitivities to be valid and agree before using a robustness label; define `robust=null` when comparison is unavailable. Either register the clip-rule × criterion combinations or narrow the wording to “agreement across the specified one-factor sensitivities.” Define whether SIGNAL and SIGNAL_STRONG are distinct outcome classes.

7. **MAJOR — M1.6: “Secondary and diagnostic arms … never gate” contradicts retained v1.0 checks.** IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES item 12 and `v3()` apply transfer-identity checks to diagnostic transfer/frozen runs with sidecars; their failures can block the verdict. Thus “every v1.0 check … unchanged” and “never gate” cannot both hold. **Fix:** state that only the additional M1 completeness/computability checks exclude secondary and diagnostic arms from gating, while inherited B1-V-3 retains its existing scope.

8. **MINOR — M1.2, M1.4–M1.6: Exact comparisons lack a fully specified numerical and missing-data contract.** Rule order, ddof and PASS-case n are specified, but accumulation order, numeric types, malformed fields, overflow and descriptive denominators remain open. Under S-excl, failure/collapse counts and survival fractions could still use either three or four clips. Partial-run exclusions can also make descriptive arm means use different populations. **Fix:** pin clip, checkpoint and seed ordering; use binary64 without intermediate rounding; reject booleans as numeric values and integers; require finite derived quantities; define malformed/missing fields as validation failures. Define collapse by `10·steps < F`, specify each sensitivity’s included clips, and report denominators and retained seed identities for every aggregate.

9. **MINOR — M1.6, M1.8–M1.9: Some governance wording can overstate what this review or a partial outcome establishes.** Saying this review “closes” the PLAN’s missing pre-seal red team risks implying that a retrospective review repairs the original procedural gap. A partial KILL can also arise from a selected subset after computability exclusions, despite the full analysis being BLOCKED. **Fix:** say this review satisfies only M1’s review requirement and leaves the original timing gap on record. Require partial outcomes to be labelled informational results on the explicitly listed retained seeds, with no inference about the complete primary comparison and no decision authority.

OVERALL: SEAL-AFTER-FIXES
