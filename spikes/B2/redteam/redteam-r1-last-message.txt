1. **MAJOR — §3, §5.5: The mask-fairness finding is not established by the cited code.** `benchmark.py` constructs every condition’s environment with `seed=0`, and the deposited attention and MLP eval logs print `Setting seed: 0` separately for all three conditions. Consequently, the claimed uninterrupted RNG progression from 0.10 into 0.20 cannot be inferred from `eval_entry` lacking a direct seed call; neither does this evidence prove identical streams across arms. **Smallest fix:** correct the reasoning, label historical mask pairing unverified, and retain the dedicated-generator procedure for P5.

2. **BLOCKER — §4.2, §7.1–§7.3: Completed-but-diverged attempts have conflicting treatment.** Inherited B0 §6.3(a) explicitly detects divergence whether or not an attempt completed, whereas B0 §3.3 can classify that attempt as completed; B2 sets `t_status=diverged` only for cells ending `DIV`. A completed attempt containing a qualifying non-finite scalar can therefore encounter B2-V-1’s BLOCKED rule instead of the promised divergence path. **Smallest fix:** define divergence independently of completion/class and explicitly specify its precedence and validity-check exemptions.

3. **MAJOR — §7.3: Multiple divergences contradict the unconditional divergence promise.** The text says a T DIV does not make the verdict FAIL, but two DIV seeds in either T arm necessarily fail B2-L-1 because DIV counts as non-passing and two passing seeds are required. **Smallest fix:** state explicitly whether that case fails learning validity or remains an unevaluable T comparison, and make the table and explanatory text agree.

4. **MAJOR — §7.2: An unevaluable T comparison can be reported as an established absence-representation advantage.** With `u_gain=true` and `t_status=diverged`, `t_gap_kept=false` automatically produces `ABSENCE_REPRESENTATION_ONLY`, although the trained control never established whether the gap survives. **Smallest fix:** give this case an explicitly unevaluable outcome or qualification while retaining `b2_gain=false`.

5. **BLOCKER — §5.1–§5.2, §7.3, §9.4: Null retention has no deterministic verdict path.** A zero mask-0 return passes the inherited finite-value checks but produces `rho_h=null`; the PLAN does not define the subsequent mean, sample SD, threshold, or comparison. Implementations could drop the seed, propagate NaN, crash, or emit NO_GAIN. **Smallest fix:** freeze a specific validity outcome for undefined primary retention, prohibit seed omission, and define null propagation for the reported statistics.

6. **MAJOR — §6: The permutation gate does not explicitly disable training masks.** Loading through `install_upstream_policy` sets a T decoder’s `auto_mask_frac=0.1`, and `.eval()` does not disable that hook; independent masks between base and permuted calls would invalidate the equivariance comparison. The stated denominator also omits the frozen smoke function’s `max(scale, 1e-12)` guard. **Smallest fix:** require `auto_mask_frac=0` for the primary check, explicit shared/permuted masks for the masked check, and the exact frozen denominator.

7. **MAJOR — §5.4, §7.2: The threshold justification overstates what the observed variability establishes.** Unpaired masks do not guarantee larger observed marginal SDs or conservative thresholds, especially with three seeds; pairing changes covariance, which the specified SE formula does not include. Likewise, the prescribed two-SE rule does not establish a calibrated significance level supporting “significantly less.” **Smallest fix:** retain the disclosed data-informed thresholds but describe them as heuristic decision margins, removing the guaranteed-conservatism and statistical-significance claims.

8. **MINOR — §7.4, §9.4, §10: Incomplete ablation reporting is underspecified.** The spend-cap rule permits incomplete `b2idx` work, but the required ablation output assumes three seeds and a computable SE; its schema has no defined `incomplete` representation. **Smallest fix:** specify the status and null fields for incomplete ablations and whether partial-run values are reported without aggregate labels.
